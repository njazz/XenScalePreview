# Privacy policy: Xen Scale Preview

Xen Scale Preview does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- It reads the `.scl` file you ask it to preview, on your device, and draws the preview there.
- It has no network access, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code.
- Files are never uploaded or copied anywhere. The app does not keep a history of the files you open.

Contact: open an issue at the project's GitHub repository.
